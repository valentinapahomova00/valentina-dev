About

Ten years in digital marketing agencies in Odesa, Ukraine: project manager, then operations director. I managed developers, designers and marketers, and along the way learned to build the sites myself, because waiting a week for a developer to move a button was too long. Client edits went from about a week to one or two days.

I work in WordPress with Elementor and in Tilda, implement Figma designs, set up analytics and automations, and use Claude Code daily for HTML/CSS fixes, specs and routine checks. Master's degree in Computer Science (WES-evaluated). Moved to Toronto in 2026, open work permit.
